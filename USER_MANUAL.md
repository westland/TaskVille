## 1. Executive Overview

**TaskVille** is a gamified, autonomous AI agent platform designed for solopreneurs, growth teams, and operators. Instead of manually typing individual chatbot prompts or wrestling with complex code pipelines, TaskVille simulates an interactive **2.5D Isometric Virtual Office** where users manage, coordinate, and observe a team of specialized AI workers executing routine business operations on complete autopilot.

TaskVille features:
- **Autonomous Task Execution:** Multi-agent swarms decompose goals into parallel workstreams, draft emails, research competitors, assemble social blitzes, design visual directions, and configure webhook relays.
- **250+ SaaS & Tool Integrations:** Instant connectivity to Gmail, Slack, HubSpot, Notion, X/Twitter, Stripe, GitHub, Shopify, and webhook endpoints.
- **Autopilot & Autonomous Scheduling:** Routine operations (morning intelligence briefings, lead triage, social content drops, competitor audits) running on automated cron schedules.
- **Visual Gamified HQ:** Real-time animated agent stations, level progression (XP), thought streams, speech bubbles, inter-agent peer reviews, and procedural cyber audio FX.
- **Google Gemini Generative AI Engine:** Live generative AI reasoning powered by Google Gemini for dynamic subtask decomposition and instant direct directives.

## 4. Interactive Controls & Office Life

### ☕ Break Lounge (Cold Brew Recharge)
- **Button:** `☕ Break` located on the top-left floating HUD.
- **Action:** Sends all agents away from their desks to the Watercooler and Coffee Bar. Plays procedural coffee brewing sound FX, updates their speech bubbles with casual office banter, and automatically returns them to their workstations after 8 seconds.

### 🎯 Strategic Sync (War Room Standup)
- **Button:** `🎯 Sync` on the floating HUD.
- **Action:** Gathers all agents into the Executive War Room. Draws animated energy delegation beams between Nova and the team, plays strategic chime SFX, and aligns all agents on primary OKRs.

### 🎨 Visual Theme Switcher
- Choose between three tailored visual aesthetics:
  1. **Cyber (Default):** Deep space navy `#070913` with electric cyan `#00f2fe` and neon glows.
  2. **Lo-Fi:** Warm neon violet and cozy ambient glow for relaxed monitoring.
  3. **Tokyo:** High-contrast retro-cyberpunk aesthetic with sharp teal and gold accents.

### 🔊 Procedural Web Audio Engine
- Built-in synthesizer generated entirely via the **Web Audio API** (zero external MP3 downloads required).
- Provides futuristic task completion chimes, mechanical typing clicks, delegation whooshes, coffee brews, and hire fanfares.
- Toggle sound on/off anytime using the speaker icon in the top header.

## 5. Mission Dispatch & Autonomous Swarms

### Launching a Swarm Mission
1. Select one of the pre-engineered quick presets or type a custom command:
   - **Q4 Growth Campaign & Social Blitz:** Research competitors, generate viral copy, create visual cards, map 7-day distribution, and arm CRM webhooks.
   - **Competitor & Pricing Teardown:** Deep crawl competitors for copy changes, feature releases, and pricing tier changes.
   - **Inbound Lead Triage & Outreach:** Scan incoming signups, score enrichment, and draft personalized outreach.
2. Click **Dispatch Swarm ⚡** or hit `Enter`.
3. The multi-agent workflow activates:
   - Nova decomposes the mission dependencies.
   - Delegation beams stream from Nova to domain specialists.
   - Specialists work simultaneously with audible keyboard clicks and sparkle particles.
   - Agents perform an automated **Inter-Agent Peer Review**, refining and cross-examining each other's outputs.
   - All participating agents gain **XP** and level up!

### Sending Direct Agent Commands (Dossier View)
1. Click any agent's avatar on the virtual floor or in the **Swarm Roster** tab.
2. The **Agent Personnel Dossier** modal opens, displaying:
   - Tasks Completed, Efficiency rating, Synergy rating, and Token bandwidth.
   - Live Thought Stream displaying real-time cognitive focus.
   - Tool badges and system behavioral persona prompt.
3. In the **Send Direct Autonomous Command** box, type a specific instruction (e.g., *"Audit our checkout funnel latency"*).
4. Click **Direct ⚡**:
   - The agent receives the directive instantly.
   - Google Gemini AI generates a live, persona-consistent response.
   - The agent executes the task, earns XP, and posts the deliverable to the Live Feed.

## 8. Executive Deliverables Vault (The Creation Repository)

The **Deliverables Vault** is the official repository for all work produced by your autonomous agents—both multi-agent swarm operations and direct custom directives (such as poems, copywriting, research briefs, or code).

### Accessing the Repository:
- **Top Navigation Bar:** Click **`📦 Deliverables`** at any time.
- **From the Live Feed:** When an agent finishes a directive, a card appears in the Live Feed with a direct button: **`[Read Full in Deliverables Vault 📦]`**. Clicking it jumps directly to that tab.
- **From the Agent Dossier:** Click **`📦 Open Deliverables Vault & Repository`** directly underneath the directive command input.

### What is Stored in the Vault:
1. **Direct Agent Creations (e.g., Poems, Letters, Code):** Whenever an agent is instructed to create a deliverable (e.g., *"Lyra, write a poem on quantum computing"*), the full text is immediately prepended as a dedicated tab: `📜 [Agent]: [Title]`.
2. **📋 Executive Summary:** Swarm KPIs, projected CAC/LTV, and milestones.
3. **🔍 Market & Intel Brief:** Competitor audits, market gaps, and positioning strategy.
4. **✍️ Copy & Sequences:** Full email sequences and viral social hooks.
5. **🎨 Art & Visual Direction:** Color hex palettes, visual rules, and diffusion prompts.
6. **🚀 Distribution Plan:** Multi-channel launch roadmap and posting windows.
7. **🔌 Tech & Webhook Spec:** JSON webhook payload schemas and integration triggers.

### Vault Actions:
- **📋 Copy Content:** Copies the active tab's formatted markdown directly to your clipboard.
- **💾 Download Markdown:** Packages all sections into a standalone `.md` export file.

## 9. Hetzner CX23 Cloud Server Deployment

Deploy TaskVille to your Hetzner CX23 cloud server (`89.167.22.10`, Ubuntu 22.04/24.04 LTS, 4GB RAM, 2 vCPU):

### Automated One-Line Setup:
```bash
# 1. SSH into your Hetzner server
ssh root@89.167.22.10

# 2. Download and execute the deployment script
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/westland/TaskVille/main/deploy-hetzner.sh | bash
```

### Manual Docker Deployment:
```bash
# Clone the repository
git clone https://github.com/westland/TaskVille.git /var/www/taskville
cd /var/www/taskville

# Create environment configuration
cat <<EOT > .env
VITE_GEMINI_API_KEY=your_gemini_api_key
VITE_APP_NAME=TaskVille
VITE_SERVER_HOST=89.167.22.10
EOT

# Build and start container in detached mode
docker compose up -d --build

# Verify container is running
docker ps
```
Your TaskVille instance will be accessible at: `http://89.167.22.10/`.
